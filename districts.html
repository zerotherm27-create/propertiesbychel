District Guides

Markets, read
at street level.

Averages hide more than they reveal. These guides record how each district actually lives, its property types, its rhythms, and the market notes that matter when real money moves.

01 · Metro Manila

Makati

The established core: gated legacy villages under mature trees, and the country's deepest market for prime condominiums. Liquidity is the quiet advantage here; good stock finds serious buyers in any season.

Legacy villages · prime condominiums · trophy penthouses

Read the Makati guide →
02 · Metro Manila

Bonifacio Global City

A planned grid that grew into the country's most walkable prime district. New-build quality leads the market, branded residences cluster here first, and leasing depth from multinationals underpins investor cases.

New-build towers · branded residences · investor units

Enquire about BGC →
03 · The South

Alabang & the South

Estate living at scale: generous lots, golf frontage, and a family rhythm the city core can't offer. The southern corridors also carry the metro's most interesting land banking conversations.

Estate lots · family compounds · golf-side villas

Enquire about the South →
04 · Visayas

Metro Cebu

The country's second market in name, and its first for coastal prime. Mactan's shoreline villas and the uptown condominium core serve different briefs; both reward local diligence on title and build quality.

Coastal villas · uptown condominiums · island investment

Enquire about Cebu →
05 · Southern Luzon

Batangas Highlands & Laguna Corridors

Weekend country within two hours of the city: cool-climate estates near Tagaytay's ridge, and growth corridors along Laguna's industrial spine for the patient investor.

Weekend estates · ridge lots · growth-corridor land

Enquire about the highlands →
06 · Mindanao

Davao

Mindanao's most orderly market: low density, strong local wealth, and prime subdivisions that rarely advertise. Entry requires relationships more than capital.

Prime subdivisions · orchard estates · city-fringe land

Enquire about Davao →
