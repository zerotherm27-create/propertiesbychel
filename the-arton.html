The Developer

Rockwell Land

Rockwell Land set out in 1995 to turn an old thermal power plant into a high-end mixed-use district; that project became Rockwell Center in Makati. The company now builds residential, retail, hotel and leisure, and office properties across the Philippines. The Arton carries that standard north, to Katipunan.
