For Sellers

Sold well is not
the same as sold fast.

A significant residence deserves a considered sale: the right story, the right photography, the right buyers, and a process that protects your privacy from first enquiry to final signature.

1

Positioning before pricing

Every property has one best story: the light, the plan, the street, the provenance. We find it before we photograph anything, and we price against true comparables rather than listed hopes. An honest guide price is a negotiating asset, not a concession.

Comparable analysis · positioning memo · pricing counsel
2

Presentation to gallery standard

Commissioned photography in natural light, considered styling, measured plans, and copy written like an essay rather than an inventory. Materials are built to hold up in front of the most discerning buyer you'd want to meet.

Photography direction · styling · plans · narrative copy
3

Discretion as strategy

Not every sale wants a public listing. Where privacy matters, the property is shown only to qualified parties under NDA, the address never appears online, and the neighbors learn of the sale when you choose to tell them.

Off-market circulation · NDA process · controlled disclosure
4

Buyers, qualified early

Viewings are reserved for parties whose capacity and seriousness have been established. You will not host tourists. Every showing comes with a briefing beforehand and candid feedback after.

Capacity checks · vetted viewings · structured feedback
5

Negotiation and completion

Offers are shaped, not just relayed: terms, timing, and certainty weighed alongside price. Documentation then moves under the eye of your counsel until transfer completes and keys change hands on your schedule.

Offer strategy · counsel coordination · completion
The best evidence of a discreet sale is that you will never read about it, including here.

The practice does not publish client names, sale figures, or testimonials.
