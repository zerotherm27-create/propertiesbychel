Foreign Buyer
Guidance

Philippine property law is particular about foreign ownership — and entirely navigable once the structures are understood. Here is the landscape in plain language, before your counsel puts it in precise language.

The Structures

What a foreign
buyer can hold

These are the routes most cross-border clients use. Which one fits depends on the asset, your residency picture, and your horizon — that's the first conversation.

Condominium title Foreign nationals may own condominium units outright, provided foreign ownership across the project stays within the constitutional 40% ceiling. The cleanest route to Philippine prime property.
Long-term lease Land itself cannot be foreign-owned, but long-term leases — commonly structured for decades, with renewal — can secure houses and lots for personal use with contractual protections.
Philippine spouse or heirs Land may be held by a Filipino spouse or by Filipino children, with estate planning shaping how interests are protected. Family-specific; take proper advice early.
Corporate structures Philippine corporations with majority Filipino ownership can hold land in defined circumstances. Legitimate when done properly, scrutinised when not — counsel is essential.
Former natural-born citizens Former Filipino citizens retain statutory rights to acquire land within area limits — often the most direct route for balikbayan buyers.

The Process

What to expect,
honestly

01

Verification comes first

Title, tax, and encumbrance checks against the registries before any commitment. Philippine diligence rewards patience; we build the timeline around it.

02

Paperwork is physical

Original documents, notarisation, and in-person steps still matter here. Powers of attorney can carry much of it if you are abroad — we coordinate the choreography.

03

Funds move formally

Inward remittances should be documented for repatriation and registration purposes. Your bank and ours will want the paper trail tidy — we keep it tidy.

04

Transfer takes weeks, not days

Taxes, clearances, and registration each have their own queue. A realistic calendar beats an optimistic one; you'll have ours in writing.

We will tell you how the market works. Your lawyer should tell you how the law applies to you — and we will gladly work alongside them.
