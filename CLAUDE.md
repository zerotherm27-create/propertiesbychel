# Properties by Chel

## Design Context

- **Register**: brand (marketing/editorial site — design IS the product). `dashboard.html` is a secondary internal admin tool, not the primary surface.
- **Platform**: web (static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step; served flat from the repo root).
- Full strategic brief: [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md). Full visual system: [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) (plus `.impeccable/design.json` for tokens/components).

Five strategic principles from PRODUCT.md — read before any design or copy work:
1. **Curation over volume** — every surface should read as selective, not exhaustive.
2. **Discretion over spectacle** — restraint is the luxury signal, not decoration.
3. **Editorial before transactional** — pages earn trust as a considered publication before they ask for anything.
4. **Close knowledge over generic coverage** — district-specific detail is the credibility mechanism.
5. **Motion should feel deliberate, never gratuitous.**

Anti-references: generic listings-aggregator UX (Zillow/Lamudi-style dense card grids), loud/flashy luxury real-estate templates (gold-drenched, gradient-heavy, autoplay video).
