## 1. What the site is today (analysis)

**Positioning, in the site's own words:** "Prime Philippine property, quietly advised." An independent practice, not a portal: few clients, both sides of the table, no volume targets, no paid placements.

**Conversion goal:** a qualified *Private Presentation* request (`presentation.html`, 3-step funnel). Fallback: Browse the Collection.

**Strengths to market from**
- A distinctive, ownable voice and visual system. Most competitors look like Lamudi; this does not.
- Real credibility assets: PRC-licensed broker *and* appraiser, 20+ years, active accreditation with 11 developers (Ayala Land, Alveo, Avida, DMCI Homes, Filinvest, Megaworld, Rockwell, Shang, Federal Land, FNG, Keen & Worth), BIR-registered operating entity (All Abode).
- Three distinct audiences already have their own pages: buyers (collection), sellers, foreign buyers.
- 10 hand-built development pages, 6 district guides, a journal.
- A personal, human promise: "You will reach a person."

**Gaps to be honest about**
| Gap | Implication |
|---|---|
| No testimonials, press, or sale figures, *by policy* ("you will never read about it, including here") | Proof must come from knowledge and craft, never from social proof. Do not "fix" this. |
| Journal has one essay; "published when there's something worth saying, not on a content calendar" | Social must feed on a slow, real editorial engine. Cadence is a ceiling, not a quota. |
| Only LinkedIn and Facebook are linked from the site (About page). No Instagram or YouTube link found | Confirm which accounts exist before launch (open item A). |
| "No mailing lists, ever" | No newsletter. Retention happens through the Journal and personal replies. |
| No phone/WhatsApp published | Do not add one in posts. Route to the presentation request or concierge@propertiesbychel.com. |
| One hero video asset (`videos/haru-tower-reel`) | Reuse sparingly; the brand rejects autoplay spectacle. |
| Analytics exists but is unverified on real data (per project notes) | Confirm `utm_*` capture before paid or tracked campaigns (open item C). |

## 2. Strategy

**Launch objective (90 days):** earn qualified private-presentation requests from serious buyers, sellers, and cross-border clients, and establish Chel as the specific, knowledgeable voice on prime Philippine districts.

**Not an objective:** follower count, reach, listing volume, "viral" anything. Success metric is request quality (see §9).

**Audiences and where they are**
| Audience | Hook | Primary channel | Landing page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic HNW buyers (Makati, BGC, Alabang, Cebu) | District knowledge, curated residences | LinkedIn, Instagram | properties / district guides |
| Owners considering a sale | "Sold well is not the same as sold fast" | LinkedIn, Facebook | sellers.html |
| Foreign / balikbayan / OFW buyers | Plain-language ownership structures | LinkedIn, Facebook | foreign-buyers.html |
| Investors / allocators | Market intelligence, leasing depth | LinkedIn | insights.html |
| Referral partners (lawyers, bankers, wealth advisers) | Composure, diligence, discretion | LinkedIn, direct | about.html |

**Content pillars** (all taken from the site's own sections)
1. **The Practice** (≈20%): Chel's four principles, how an engagement runs, what discretion means in practice.
2. **Districts, street by street** (≈30%): Makati, BGC, Alabang & the South, Cebu, Batangas/Laguna, Davao. Walked knowledge, not averages.
3. **Foreign Buyer Guidance** (≈20%): structures, process, honest timelines. Always with the not-legal-advice line.
4. **Seller craft** (≈15%): positioning, presentation, qualified viewings, off-market handling. No client names, no figures.
5. **The Collection** (≈15%): a single residence or development, written like an essay, facts verified.

**Core message hierarchy:** (1) We know this district specifically. (2) Representation here means judgement and discretion. (3) The collection is selective. (4) A private presentation is worth the small friction of asking.

## 3. Brand voice and visual guardrails (non-negotiable)

### Voice
Quiet, discreet, considered, editorial. Confident without selling. Plain words, short declaratives, a dry understated wit ("including the obligation to agree with us", "Time zones are our problem, not yours"). First person plural for the practice ("we"), first person singular only when Chel speaks as herself.

**Signature moves lifted from the site**
- Name the opposite and decline it: "A practice, not a portal." "Sold well is not the same as sold fast."
- A parenthetical that adds a human specific: "(a family seat, a rebalanced portfolio, a discreet sale)".
- Say what it costs, not just what it offers: "what each address really offers, and what it quietly costs."
- Honest about limits: "Sometimes the best advice is to wait." "A realistic calendar beats an optimistic one."
- Close with an invitation, never a push: "Begin quietly." "Request a private presentation."

**Preferred vocabulary:** quietly, considered, discretion, advice, judgement, practice, residence, walked, street-by-street, particulars, brief, mandate, composure, "worth your attention". British-leaning spelling as on the site: *judgement, neighbourhood, enquiry, notarisation, recognised*.

**Never use:** stunning, breathtaking, dream home, luxurious/luxury (let the facts carry it), exclusive, must-see, amazing, hot deal, don't miss out, limited time, act now, DM for price, "investment opportunity of a lifetime", guaranteed returns, appreciation promises, exclamation marks, ALL CAPS, emoji, hashtag walls, "link in bio!!".

**Formatting rules:** max 3 hashtags (lowercase, specific: `#makati`, `#privatepresentation`). No emoji. Sentence case. Prices only as "Price on application" unless the listing page shows one. Never publish a private address; location is a locator ("High Street South corridor").

### Visual (from DESIGN.md)
- Palette: Ink Navy `#142B5A`, Deep Espresso Navy `#0B1D3A`, Warm Paper (≈ `#F8F5EF`), Antique Brass `#D4AF37` as a **hairline only** (rules, corner brackets, a small label). Never a fill, never gold-drenched, no gradients, no shadows, no rounded corners.
- Type: Playfair Display 500 for names and headlines; Work Sans for everything else; labels in uppercase with 0.22em tracking.
- Signature motif: square photo frames with 1px brass corner brackets (top-left, bottom-right).
- Logos: `images/logo-navy.png` on light, `images/logo-white.png` on navy.
- Motion (video/reels): slow, deliberate, no whip-pans, no trending audio, no autoplay-style spectacle. Reduced pacing over energy.
- Photography: natural light, no HDR, no stock "luxury", no people posing with keys.

### Compliance rules that follow from the brand
- Sellers: never post client names, sale prices, "sold" announcements, or testimonials. The site promises this.
- Foreign buyers: every structure post carries "General information, not legal or tax advice. Take independent Philippine counsel."
- Do not share leads' or clients' details, even anonymised if identifiable.
- Verify before posting whether PRC (RA 9646) and DHSUD/developer-project advertising rules require a license number or project license number on ads. This plan does not assume the answer (open item D).

## 4. Channel plan

| Channel | Role | Cadence (ceiling) | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (Chel personal profile `aremchelcruzado` leads; no company page needed at launch) | Authority, referral partners, investors, foreign buyers | 2 posts/week | Text essays, 5-8 slide carousels |
| **Facebook** (`propertiesbychelcruzado`) | Domestic buyers/sellers, OFW and balikbayan reach, existing audience | 2 posts/week | Same essays adapted, album posts, one reel/month |

Cadence is deliberately light. The site says the Journal publishes "when there's something worth saying, not on a content calendar." Treat §6 as a *bank of ready work*; skip any slot where the post wouldn't be worth reading.

## 5. Phased launch

### Phase 0: Foundation (Week -2 to 0)
Checklist, in order:
1. Confirm accounts and handles; set bio, logo, header in brand palette (open item A).
2. Bio line (all channels): *"Private real estate advisory. Prime Philippine property, quietly advised. Makati City."* Link: `propertiesbychel.com`.
3. Add `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` to every outbound link; confirm the first-party analytics records them (open item C).
4. Check Open Graph preview of `index`, `foreign-buyers`, `sellers`, and 3 development pages by pasting each URL into the Facebook Sharing Debugger and LinkedIn Post Inspector (listing share cards already ship via `middleware.js`; verify these static pages).
5. Publish **two more Journal essays** before launch so the Journal isn't a single post (draft prompts in §8, Prompt 7). Real content only.
6. Build the photo/plate library: 12 square plates + 6 district portraits in the brand frame.
7. Re-record or re-cut `videos/haru-tower-reel` only if it follows the motion rules.
8. Pin posts (see Week 1).

### Phase 1: Introduction (Weeks 1-2): "Who we are and how we work"
Goal: establish the practice and the voice. No listings yet.
- Post 1: Launch statement (The Practice).
- Post 2: "A practice, not a portal." (principles carousel).
- Post 3: First district essay (Makati).
- Post 4: Foreign-buyer structures post 1 of 3.
- Pin Post 1 on LinkedIn and Facebook.

### Phase 2: Depth (Weeks 3-6): "Districts and structures"
Goal: demonstrate specific knowledge. One district + one foreign-buyer + one seller-craft piece per fortnight, then a collection post.
- Introduce first Collection post (one residence, essay-length).
- Start a recurring series: **"What it quietly costs"** (one district, one honest trade-off).

### Phase 3: Quiet conversion (Weeks 7-12)
Goal: convert attention to presentation requests without pressure.
- Seller-craft series: "The mandate conversation."
- Cross-border series: "From anywhere": video-call-first briefs.
- Partner outreach: short personal notes to 10-15 lawyers, wealth advisers, and bankers with the foreign-buyer and seller pages. One note each, no sequences.
- Review §9; double down on the pillar that produces requests, drop the rest.

## 9. Measurement (quality over volume)

Review fortnightly; never report vanity totals alone.
| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Private presentation requests, by source (UTM) and by brief type (acquire / sell / lease / invest / unsure) | The only true goal |
| Share of requests with a stated range of ≥ ₱50M and a timeframe | Qualification quality |
| Replies/DMs from named, relevant professionals (lawyers, advisers, developers) | Referral-network health |
| Visits to foreign-buyers.html, sellers.html, district guides from social | Which pillar earns real attention |
| Saves and shares (LinkedIn/Instagram) over likes | Signals of considered interest |
Do not track or optimise: follower counts, impressions as a goal, posting frequency.
