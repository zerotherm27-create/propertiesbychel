About

The judgement behind
the signature.

Chel Cruzado is a PRC-licensed real estate broker and appraiser with more than two decades of experience across operations, sales, marketing, and business management.

That background — spanning corporate operations and entrepreneurship — shapes a practical, numbers-first approach to property decisions: market research, valuation insight, and financing guidance combined so a decision holds up under real scrutiny, not just at first impression. Through Properties by Chel, that expertise is focused on the Philippines' prime residential market — acquisitions, considered sales, and the advisory work in between.

Philosophy

Four working principles

I

Advice before inventory

The first task is understanding what you should buy or how you should sell — not pushing what happens to be available. Sometimes the best advice is to wait.

II

Discretion as default

Names, addresses, and intentions stay private. Off-market work is handled off market, and nothing about your affairs becomes marketing material.

III

The market, in the particular

Prime property is a street-by-street market. Advice here rests on walked knowledge of specific buildings, villages, and corridors — not on headline averages.

IV

Composure at the table

Negotiation is preparation plus patience. Positions are built early, pressure is absorbed calmly, and the client's leverage is spent only where it counts.
