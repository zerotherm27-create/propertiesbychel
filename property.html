The Zenith Penthouse

BGC, Taguig · 620 sqm interior · 4 bedrooms + staff suiteGuide ₱320M

The Residence

A single floor,
held above the city.

The Zenith occupies its tower's entire crown, one household, one lift lobby, no shared walls. The great room runs the full western elevation under five-metre ceilings, so the skyline reads as a mural that resets every evening.

The plan divides cleanly: entertaining to the west, family to the east, service circulation kept invisible. A gallery corridor was engineered for hanging weight, a rarity in local towers, and the primary suite takes the quiet southern corner with its own study.

Building services run to hotel standard, and the residence has been maintained by a single owner since turnover. Viewing is unhurried and by appointment, with financials available under NDA.

The Setting

Quietest corner of
a walkable city.

The residence sits on the calmer southern rim of BGC's grid, close enough to walk to High Street's restaurants and the international schools, far enough that weekend mornings stay quiet.

Access favours principals who move often: 20–30 minutes to NAIA terminals outside peak, direct arteries to Makati's CBD, and hospital-grade medical care within the district.
