A Connected Address

Makati Southwest Gateway

Chino Roces Avenue is being remodelled by the Makati City Government into a mixed-use zone of residential and commercial space, with portions to be redeveloped as the Venir, Central, and Cielo zones. Fortis Residences is organically connected to the Makati CBD, with access to BGC and Pasay City.

Developer showroomBangkal, Makati
Developer showroomOak Harbor Residences, Parañaque

Showrooms are at 1321 Apolinario St., Brgy. Bangkal, Makati City, and at Oak Harbor Residences on Jackson Avenue, Asiaworld, Parañaque City.
